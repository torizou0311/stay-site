Floor plan

Please check before booking

  • There is no elevator. The stairs are narrow, so large suitcases are hard to carry (Rooms 201 and 202 are on the 2nd floor).
  • There is an uphill walk from JR Tsurumi Station, which can be tiring with heavy luggage.
  • The ceiling of the loft is low. Tall guests should watch their heads.
  • The edge of the loft bed has no guard rail. Please take care if you travel with small children.
  • A frosted-glass skylight lets in morning light (eye masks are provided).
  • There is no bathtub, only a shower room.
  • TV broadcasts cannot be watched (connect a PC or game console to the monitor).

Access

Getting here

  • About 11 min on foot from JR Tsurumi Station (West Exit). There is an uphill section.
  • About 17 min on foot from Keikyu Tsurumi Station.
  • About 3 min by taxi from JR Tsurumi Station.
  • Taxi fare between the station and the accommodation is reimbursed up to 1,000 yen one way / 2,000 yen round trip.

Location: Teraya, Tsurumi-ku, Yokohama. The full address is provided once your booking is confirmed.

Train times from Tsurumi Station

  • Kawasaki: about 4 min
  • Yokohama: about 10 min
  • Tokyo: about 25 min
  • Haneda Airport: about 20 min

Times are from Tsurumi Station, not from the accommodation.

Please check before booking

  • There is no elevator. The stairs are narrow, so large suitcases are hard to carry (Rooms 201 and 202 are on the 2nd floor).
  • There is an uphill walk from JR Tsurumi Station, which can be tiring with heavy luggage.
  • The ceiling of the loft is low. Tall guests should watch their heads.
  • The edge of the loft bed has no guard rail. Please take care if you travel with small children.
  • A frosted-glass skylight lets in morning light (eye masks are provided).
  • There is no bathtub, only a shower room.
  • TV broadcasts cannot be watched (connect a PC or game console to the monitor).

FAQ

How do I check in?

Check-in is self check-in with an electronic lock, from 16:00. Check-out is by 10:00.

Is there parking?

There is no parking on the premises. Reservable parking lots and coin parking lots are nearby.

Is the taxi from the station free?

We reimburse the taxi fare between the station and the accommodation up to 1,000 yen one way and 2,000 yen round trip.

How many guests can stay?

Each room accommodates up to 3 guests.

Is smoking allowed?

No. Smoking is not allowed anywhere on the premises.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes. Free Wi-Fi is available in every room.

Can I leave my luggage?

Before check-in, we may be able to keep your luggage if the room is free; please ask in advance. We cannot keep luggage after check-out.

Is there cleaning or towel change during my stay?

There is no cleaning or towel change during your stay. Please wash the towels in the washing machine in your room.

Is there anything I need to do after booking?

By law, the name, address, occupation and other details of every guest must be registered through our online form after booking. Guests without an address in Japan also need to submit a passport image.

Yokohama Gate Retreat

A calm hilltop stay within walking distance of Yokohama Station

Stay like a local in the heart of Yokohama

About a 15-minute walk from JR Yokohama Station, in a calm residential neighborhood on a hill, a little away from the busy station area. Minato Mirai, K-Arena Yokohama and Chinatown are all close by. Enjoy sightseeing and concerts to the fullest, then come back to a room where you can really rest. Free parking is available (reservation required). Access

Getting here

  • About 15 minutes on foot from Yokohama Station (Kita-West / West Exit; JR, private railways and subway). The second half is uphill.
  • About 11 minutes on foot from Tammachi Station (Tokyu Toyoko Line), with a gentle uphill.
  • About 12 minutes on foot from Mitsuzawa-shimocho Station (Yokohama Municipal Subway Blue Line).

Location: Sawatari, Kanagawa-ku, Yokohama. The exact address is shared after your booking is confirmed. Please come from the west side of Yokohama Station; the East Exit is a long way round.

By train from Yokohama Station

  • Minato Mirai: about 3 min
  • Motomachi-Chukagai (Chinatown): about 9 min
  • Shin-Yokohama (Shinkansen): about 11 min
  • Shinagawa: about 18 min
  • Tokyo: about 25 min
  • Shibuya: about 27 min
  • Haneda Airport: about 25–33 min
  • Kamakura: about 24 min

Approximate times from Yokohama Station (not from the property). From Tammachi Station, Minato Mirai (about 5 min) and Motomachi-Chukagai (about 12 min) are direct, with no transfer.

Parking

Parking is free of charge. Spaces are limited, so advance reservation is required.

The neighborhood

  • A calm residential area on a hill, a little away from the station crowds
  • Sawatari Chuo Park, with a wide lawn and playground, is about 3 minutes on foot
  • A small supermarket and convenience stores are nearby
  • The shops, underground mall and restaurants at Yokohama Station West Exit are about 13–15 minutes on foot
  • K-Arena Yokohama: about 14 minutes on foot from Yokohama Station (about 30 minutes on foot from the property)
  • Pia Arena MM, Pacifico Yokohama and Zepp: about 10–15 minutes by train or bus from Yokohama Station
  • NHK Spring Mitsuzawa Football Stadium: about 25 minutes on foot
  • Kanagawa-dai Sekimon site on the old Tokaido road: about 8 minutes on foot

Please check before booking

  • You need to climb an outdoor staircase from the street to the building entrance (the stairs shown in the exterior photo). Please keep this in mind if you have large luggage or difficulty with stairs.
  • There is no elevator; access is by stairs only (Room 305 is on the 3rd floor). Please keep this in mind if you have large luggage.
  • The walk from the stations to the property is uphill, which can be tiring with heavy luggage.
  • The TV has no tuner, so Japanese broadcast channels are not available (Netflix is available).
  • Parking is free, but spaces are limited and must be reserved in advance.
